Azazel: The Fallen Angel

By AncientKnowledgeHub • Published: March 2026 • Category: Watchers • 4 min read

Azazel is a figure from ancient Jewish and Christian apocryphal texts, often identified as one of the fallen angels. He is described as an angel who descended to Earth and taught humans forbidden knowledge.

Azazel is also associated with the teaching of metallurgy, astronomy, and other sciences to humans.

Famous for his role in the apocryphal texts, Azazel's story serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of seeking forbidden knowledge and the consequences of defying divine authority.
