Who Were the Watchers in the Book of Enoch?

By AncientKnowledgeHub • Published: March 2026 • Category: Watchers • 4 min read

The Watchers are described in the ancient Book of Enoch as angels who descended to Earth and shared forbidden knowledge with humanity. Their actions changed the course of human history and led to the birth of the Nephilim giants.

According to the ancient text, these angels took human wives and taught humans secrets of warfare, sorcery, and advanced knowledge. Their rebellion ultimately resulted in divine judgment and the great flood.

Many ancient traditions describe similar beings who passed forbidden knowledge to humanity, raising questions about the origins of ancient civilization.
