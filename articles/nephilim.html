The Nephilim Giants

By AncientKnowledgeHub • Published: March 2026 • Category: Watchers • 4 min read

The Nephilim are described in the ancient Book of Enoch as the offspring of the Watchers (angels) and human women. They are portrayed as mighty warriors and giants who roamed the earth before the great flood.

Their existence is a testament to the complex relationships between divine and human realms in ancient belief systems.

The Nephilim's story serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of bridging the gap between divine and human realms, and the consequences of defying divine authority.
